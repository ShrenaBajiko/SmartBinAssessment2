    What the model sees & diagnostics

    Used by the image classifier pipeline (for example your Teachable Machine model), and by the “centre region” fallback. Only the area inside the dashed box is classified, so the item should fill most of it.

    The self-test checks that the bundled model computes correctly on this device's compute backend.

    Classifier configuration

    COCO-SSD (Apache-2.0, 9 MB) finds everyday objects such as bottles, cups, fruit, books and phones. The largest object's label decides the bin (see Bin mapping below). People and furniture are ignored. Items COCO does not know (cans, boxes, crumpled paper, batteries) are not detected.

    Each class is mapped to a bin under Bin mapping. Class names such as recycling, landfill, organics or yellow are mapped automatically; a class named unknown or background shows “No item”.

    EcoVision MobileNetV3-Large (MIT licence, by AmadFR) trained on about 20,000 images: battery, biological, cardboard, clothes, glass, metal, paper, plastic, shoes, trash. 8.4 MB download.

    MobileNetV2 (0.5 width, 160×160) trained on TrashNet: cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic, trash. 1.4 MB and about 5× less computation. Needs one item on a plain background; tends to over-predict cardboard/paper.

      Accepts TensorFlow.js layers or graph models, including a Teachable Machine “TensorFlow.js” export. The files stay on this device; they are not uploaded to any server.

      Bin mapping: label → bin (editable)

      Defaults follow common Australian kerbside rules and are defined in bins.js. Councils differ, so adjust them here; changes are saved on this device and highlighted.
